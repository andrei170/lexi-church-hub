VSL Preview Script - Lexi Church

Built: 2026-05-16 Format: 90-second cohort intro reel preview. Shot on Lexi. Hero of the cohort landing page once filmed. Vertical IG-native format (9:16) for the IG primary use, converted to 16:9 web embed for the landing-page hero. Production note: This is the short preview spec, not a full 8-minute long-form VSL. Production version of long-form ships after the first cohort cycle proves conversion. Until the production reel is filmed, the demo landing uses a placeholder card with a "Real Take" pull-quote.

Spec

  • Length: 90 seconds, hard cap at 100
  • Format: Direct-to-camera, talking-head, eye-level framing
  • Setting: Lexi in a clean office or home-office environment. Warm-neutral palette, natural daylight if possible. Operator-grounded, not styled-pitch-deck. Avoid "luxury-real-estate" tropes (sweeping kitchen-island B-roll, chandelier backdrops) - the visual matches the operator-grounded leadership voice rule.
  • Wardrobe: Professional-warm. What she would wear to a listing presentation or a WCR board meeting. Avoid "influencer-lifestyle" wardrobe cues.
  • Lighting: Soft key + fill. Natural daylight is fine if controlled. Avoid hard rim light, avoid neon backdrops, avoid heavy ring-light glare.
  • Audio: Lav mic or shotgun. Quiet room. No background music.
  • Lower-third (on-screen graphic): "Lexi Church - @lexichurchre - Local President at Creed Realty - First VP, Women's Council of REALTORS - Founder, FLOH Realty Group" appears at 0:03 for 5 seconds.

Script

[0:00 - 0:08] HOOK

If you are a real estate agent in Virginia or North Carolina and you have been watching my content, this is the install version of the playbook I have been running across my first eight years in the lane.

[0:08 - 0:22] CREDENTIAL

For context. I am an Associate Broker, dual-licensed in Virginia and North Carolina. I am Local President at Creed Realty in Virginia Beach. I am First Vice President of Women's Council of REALTORS. I founded FLOH Realty Group (For the Love of Homes), the team I lead. I am Military Relocation Professional certified. I have closed over $28 million in stated career sales across the 757 metro and the NC border counties.

[0:22 - 0:38] THE GAP

Most newer VA and NC agents I talk to have two endpoints in their plan. Watch the leadership content for the credential story at the top. Hope that the lead-gen system, the listing-presentation playbook, the contract-negotiation discipline, and the niche calendar somehow come together in the brokerage onboarding at the bottom. There is no middle. No structured way to map the lead-gen tiers, write the listing-presentation playbook, rep the contract-negotiation order, or build the MRP-credentialed military-relocation lane in the order that actually compounds.

[0:38 - 0:55] THE COHORT

I built a cohort to fill that middle. Two tracks. Track A for the licensed agent in years 1 to 3, 8 weeks, foundation through first-leadership-ladder. Track B for the mid-tenure agent at years 4 to 8 moving toward team ownership, 8 weeks, solo-to-team-build through brand-architecture. Both run with me on weekly live calls, office hours, and a cohort room you keep beyond graduation. Caps at 20 seats on Track A, 15 on Track B. Four cycles per year.

[0:55 - 1:10] OUTCOMES

By the end of Track A you have a written lead-generation system mapped to your geography, a listing-presentation playbook drafted, a contract-negotiation framework, an MRP-niche calendar laid against your local installations, and a written 12-month execution plan reviewed by me on the 1:1 call. By the end of Track B you have a written team-build plan, a brokerage-relationship architecture mapped, a recruiting playbook drafted, a brand-architecture document, and a written 12-month team-build execution plan reviewed on the 1:1 call.

[1:10 - 1:25] PRICING + COMPLIANCE

Pricing sits between the existing online + in-person classes at the bottom of the ladder and bespoke 1:1 work at the top. The cohort is general real estate agent education and leadership coaching. It is brokerage-agnostic. It does not function as a recruiting funnel for any specific brokerage. Not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice. Results vary by market, capital, and operator execution.

[1:25 - 1:30] CTA

If you are licensed in Virginia or North Carolina, the application is on the page below. Apply, and I will see you on week one.

Captioning

Burn-in captions required. Most viewers watch on mobile with sound off in the first 3-5 seconds of the landing-page autoplay. Caption styling: clean sans-serif, white text with subtle drop shadow, positioned in the lower third above any lower-third graphic. The compliance line at 1:10 to 1:24 needs to be legible on mobile - high-contrast captioning, no animation distractions on that segment.

Open and close frames

Open: First frame is Lexi sitting calmly, hands visible, mid-sentence. Avoid a wide intro shot or a logo card - the first 1.5 seconds should be voice + face only to hold the autoplay viewer.

Close: Final frame is Lexi holding eye contact with the camera at "see you on week one." Cut to the page CTA below without a logo bumper. Logo-bumpers cost 2-3 seconds of attention at the moment the viewer is closest to clicking.
