Architecture

Funnel Blueprint - Lexi Church

Built: 2026-05-16 Frame: Full funnel architecture for The 757 Agent Cohort, with two parallel tracks (First Three Years In The Lane + From Solo Producer To Team Owner) feeding the Typeform application gate. Plus the two scorecards (Agent Leadership Readiness Score for the agent-side audience, 757 Home Sale Readiness Score for the consumer-side audience) feeding both the cohort funnel and the FLOH Realty Group brokerage intake.

Stage-by-stage detail

Stage 1 - Top of funnel

Surfaces: Instagram reels (the dominant), Instagram carousels, Instagram stories, Facebook page, LinkedIn leadership thread, WCR + Creed Realty industry referrals.

Goal: Pull warm audience attention into the funnel via two converting assets (one for the agent slice, one for the consumer slice).

Mechanics: Every piece of content includes a single CTA, routed by audience slice:

  • Agent-leadership content (Local President + WCR + team-build + recruitment) routes to the Agent Leadership Readiness Score
  • Consumer market-commentary content ("Real Take" reels + 757 micro-market content + military-relocation content) routes to the 757 Home Sale Readiness Score
  • LinkedIn leadership thread routes to the Agent Leadership Readiness Score
  • WCR + Creed Realty industry surfaces route to the Agent Leadership Readiness Score

Tracking: UTM-tagged scorecard links from every IG bio link, every reel description, every Facebook post, every LinkedIn post, every story. Plausible or GA4 on both scorecard pages, segmented by source.

Stage 2 - Scorecard A ("Agent Leadership Readiness Score")

Format: 12 questions, four dimensions (lead generation / listing presentation / niche calendar / operator inputs), 90 seconds to complete.

Output: 0-100 readiness score, top-three-gaps summary, email-captured score report.

Tool stack options:

  • ScoreApp (purpose-built for scorecard quizzes, native scoring engine)
  • Typeform (consistent with the cohort application form)
  • Outgrow (alternative with more design flexibility)

Email integration: scoring tool pushes email + score band + top-three-gap tags + years-licensed band to the ESP. Quiz completion triggers the score-report email + entry into the agent-side nurture sequence.

Segmentation tags applied:

  • Score band: 0-40 (foundation), 41-70 (in-build), 71-100 (advanced)
  • Years-licensed band: 0-1 / 1-3 / 4-8 / 8-plus
  • Geography: VA / NC / both
  • Team status: solo / team-member / team-leader
  • Niche focus: military-relocation / first-time-buyer / luxury / move-up / generalist

Stage 3 - Scorecard B ("757 Home Sale Readiness Score")

Format: 10 questions, three dimensions (home equity + timeline / motivation + financing / micro-market clarity), 90 seconds to complete.

Output: 0-100 readiness score, top-three-gaps summary, email-captured score report. High-readiness respondents (score 70-plus) get a routing CTA to the FLOH Realty Group brokerage intake form.

Tool stack options:

  • ScoreApp (purpose-built, native scoring)
  • Typeform (consistent with the cohort application form)
  • Outgrow (alternative)

Email integration: scoring tool pushes email + score band + timeline band + financing band to the ESP. Quiz completion triggers the score-report email + entry into the consumer-side nurture sequence. Score 70-plus respondents also trigger a Slack notification to the FLOH ops contact for prompt brokerage outreach.

Segmentation tags applied:

  • Score band: 0-40 (early-research), 41-70 (active-prep), 71-100 (transaction-ready)
  • Timeline band: 6-plus-months / 3-6-months / under-3-months
  • Motivation band: move-up / downsize / relocation / military-orders / investment
  • Micro-market: Virginia Beach / Norfolk / Suffolk / Chesapeake / Hampton / Newport News / NC border county

Stage 4 - Email nurture (5-email cold-first-touch sequence, agent side)

Cadence: Days 0, 2, 5, 8, 12 from quiz completion.

Routing: the sequence is delivered to all completions of Scorecard A. The CTA in the final email is conditional on score band - low scores route to Track A (foundation), middle-to-high scores route to Track B (team-build), or to a "both might fit" follow-up that lets the lead self-select.

Detail of the sequence lives in email-sequence.

Stage 5 - Cohort landing page

Surfaces:

  • Combined cohort landing (Track A + Track B side by side) - the primary
  • Track-specific deep links once the cohort proves conversion - split into two surfaces in cycle 2 or 3

Build choice (for the demo): ship one combined cohort landing that holds both tracks side-by-side. The post-install version splits into two surfaces once we know which track pulls harder.

Hero element: for the demo, a placeholder card stating "Cohort intro reel embeds on Day 1 of install" - because the production reel has not been filmed yet, and Lexi does not currently operate a personal YouTube channel for direct oEmbed. Production version swaps to either (a) a freshly-filmed 90-second cohort-intro reel by Lexi (vertical IG-native format converted to web), or (b) an embed of her single highest-performing Instagram reel re-uploaded to a Lexi-owned YouTube short for stable oEmbed once we lock the cohort framing.

Conversion gate: Typeform application (uTtFirS4). Application reads as: which track, current years licensed, current production volume band, current team status, why now. Manual review on submit, approval triggers a Stripe or Stan store payment link.

Stage 6 - Application + checkout

Flow:

  1. Applicant submits Typeform
  2. Submission routes to Zapier or Make.com webhook
  3. Webhook fires three actions: - Creates contact record in ConvertKit / Customer.io / GHL (chosen CRM) - Tags contact with cohort track + years-licensed band + production-volume band + team status - Adds applicant to a #cohort-review Slack channel for manual review (Lexi or her ops contact reviews inside 48 hours)
  4. On approval, applicant receives either (a) a direct payment link via email, or (b) a calendar invite to the post-application consult slot
  5. Payment triggers: - Cohort welcome email with calendar invite to first call - Add to private cohort room (Circle or Slack) - Add to cohort-active segment in ESP for in-cohort comms

Integration points (the load-bearing wiring)

  1. Scorecard A completion -> ESP entry + tag application (agent-side). Single point of failure for the cohort funnel.
  2. Scorecard B completion -> ESP entry + tag application (consumer-side) + Slack notification if score 70-plus. Single point of failure for the brokerage routing.
  3. Email nurture -> cohort landing CTA -> Typeform. Typeform fires a webhook on submit.
  4. Typeform webhook -> CRM + Slack review channel + status: pending-review.
  5. Manual approval -> Stripe payment link -> Stripe webhook -> cohort onboarding email + Circle invite + status: enrolled.
  6. Cohort completion -> alumni status -> ongoing comms segment.

Every transition is webhook-driven, every transition is logged, every transition is monitored for failure with email alerts to Lexi's ops contact.

Install phasing

Days 1-3 - Core funnel install:

  • Cohort landing page deployed (single combined page, both tracks)
  • Typeform application live with webhook (uTtFirS4)
  • Stripe products created for Track A and Track B
  • Brand colours locked (one of the small Day-1 fixes)

Days 4-7 - Capture + nurture layer:

  • Both scorecard quizzes built and live on their own subdomains
  • 5-email cold-first-touch nurture sequence loaded into ESP (agent-side, with branching to consumer-side as needed)
  • Tag-driven segmentation logic live
  • Pixel + GA4 firing on all surfaces
  • UTM tagging convention documented and rolled out across IG bio, Facebook, LinkedIn, bio-link router

Days 8-30 - Cohort launch:

  • Track A cohort 1 application window opens
  • IG bio-link router updated: cohort landing as primary for agent slice, scorecard A as secondary capture, scorecard B as the consumer-side capture
  • Inbound leads worked through nurture
  • First cohort fills, starts week 1 by Day 30
  • Existing free-call-style slot continues as the post-application consult for high-readiness applicants

Days 30-90 - Scale + iterate:

  • Track B cohort 1 launches
  • Classes catalogue productised: Day-30 onward, the existing online + in-person classes line gets a public catalogue with set dates, prices, seat caps, and a Calendly + Stripe or Eventbrite booking layer
  • Ad spend (Meta + LinkedIn) starts on top-performing organic IG reels and the leadership-thread LinkedIn posts
  • Cohort 2 (Track A) waitlist opens

What this funnel is not

  • Not a course launch. No 7-day launch sequence, no countdown timer, no $97 evergreen tripwire. Cohort-anchored, quarterly cadence, capped enrolment.
  • Not a paid-ads-first build. Organic warm traffic from the existing 21K IG audience plus the LinkedIn leadership thread plus the WCR + Creed Realty network carries the first 2-3 cohort fills. Paid spend joins after the funnel has proven conversion at cold-to-warm rates.
  • Not a brokerage-recruiting funnel. The cohort is brokerage-agnostic agent education. It does not function as a Creed Realty recruiting layer.
  • Not a regulated real-estate or financial-advice service. The cohort teaches general real estate agent education and leadership coaching. It does not provide personalised real-estate, legal, tax, or financial advice. Not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice. Results vary by market, capital, and operator execution.

Post-application consult repositioning sequence

The cohort uses a post-application consult slot (the equivalent of the existing free-call hook, but repositioned). The repositioning sequence:

  1. Build the Typeform application (uTtFirS4) as the cohort-application gate
  2. Connect the Typeform to ConvertKit / Customer.io (or chosen ESP) so every submission tags the contact
  3. Build the cohort landing as the bio-link primary for the agent slice
  4. Configure the consult slot so approved applicants who want to talk before paying get the slot - not unapproved applicants
  5. Fire the Stripe + GA4 + Meta pixel onto the cohort landing
  6. Update IG bio, Facebook page, LinkedIn featured-section, and the bio-link router to route to the scorecards first, the cohort landing second

Total install time on the consult-slot repositioning: 2-3 days, parallel to the cohort landing and the scorecards.
