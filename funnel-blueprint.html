﻿# Funnel Blueprint - Lexi Church / FLOH Realty Group Date: 2026-05-17 Funnel Type: Military-Niche Lead Generation

Funnel Overview

Goal: Convert IG followers and organic search traffic into email subscribers, then into consult calls with Lexi.

Primary audience: Military families PCSing to Hampton Roads (Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Suffolk, Hampton, Newport News). Secondary: general Hampton Roads buyers and sellers.

Lead magnet: "Hampton Roads PCS Move Guide for Military Families" - free PDF download covering BAH rates, VA loan process, base-proximity neighborhood guide, and PCS timeline checklist.

Stage 1 - Traffic (IG Content)

Triggers: Reels and Carousels on military-niche content (see content-strategy.md). Stories with "Link in bio" or swipe-up CTA. Consistent MRP/PCS-focused hashtag use (#757realtor #Hamptonroads #PCSmove #militaryrelocation #NorfolkNavalStation #VirginiaBeachRealtor).

Target audience signals: Follows military accounts, base pages, PCS/military spouse groups. Virginia Beach/Norfolk/Hampton Roads geo.

Stage 2 - Landing Page

URL: lexichurch.com/military (or dedicated page)

Headline: "Hampton Roads Real Estate. Built for Military Families."

Sub: "PCS orders? VA loan? Not sure where to start? Lexi Church is Hampton Roads' MRP-certified REALTOR - dual-licensed VA + NC, 8 years full-time, and she knows this market from the base gates out."

Lead magnet box: "Download: Hampton Roads PCS Move Guide for Military Families" - name + email capture + "Send me the guide" CTA.

Trust signals: MRP badge, dual-state license, 8 yrs, $28M+, 21K IG, FLOH Realty Group.

Secondary CTA: "Or book a call directly" for high-intent visitors.

Stage 3 - Lead Magnet Delivery

Email subject: "Your Hampton Roads PCS Move Guide is here"

Body: Brief welcome. Link to PDF download. Set expectation for the follow-up sequence ("Over the next two weeks I'll send you the things I tell every military family before they start looking in Hampton Roads.").

PDF content (8-12 pages): - Welcome from Lexi + FLOH intro - 2026 BAH rates by rank for VA Beach/Norfolk Military Housing Area - VA loan process: what military buyers need to know - Hampton Roads installation map + neighborhood guide by base - PCS timeline checklist (90/60/30/14/7 days out) - What to expect at closing in Virginia - Lexi's contact info + book-a-call link

Stage 4 - Email Nurture Sequence

5 emails over 14 days (see email-sequence.md for full scripts).

  • Email 1 (Day 0): PDF delivery + welcome
  • Email 2 (Day 2): "The one VA loan mistake I see military buyers make in Hampton Roads"
  • Email 3 (Day 5): "The 5 neighborhoods closest to NAS Norfolk/NAS Oceana - and what each one is actually like"
  • Email 4 (Day 9): "How to buy a home in Hampton Roads when you have 60 days and PCS orders"
  • Email 5 (Day 14): "Ready to talk? Here's what a free call with Lexi actually looks like" - hard CTA to consult booking

Stage 5 - Consult to Close

Consult call (20 min, free): Lexi qualifies buyer/seller, assesses timeline and VA loan status, gives market context. Books second working session.

Conversion to representation: Buyer representation agreement or listing agreement. Transaction routed through FLOH Realty Group at Creed Realty.

Metrics to Track

  • IG reel reach and bio link clicks (weekly)
  • Landing page conversion rate (email captures / unique visits - target 30%+)
  • Email open rates (target 40%+ for military niche)
  • Consult call bookings per month
  • Deals sourced from PCS funnel (tag in CRM)
