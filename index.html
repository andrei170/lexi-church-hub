The Diagnostic
02

The Agent Leadership Readiness Score - Capture The Agent-Side Audience Into A Tracked Email List

There is no visible Lexi-owned email list. The bio link on Instagram, the contact line on Facebook, the LinkedIn headline - none of them capture an email into a tracked, segmented list owned by you. There is no quiz, no scorecard, no PDF download for the agent-side audience. Every IG follower who watches a leadership reel, gets pulled in by the Local President plus First VP plus founder framing, and lands on the bio link exits the funnel without entering a list that can be nurtured back into the cohort. The fix is short. An "Agent Leadership Readiness Score" - a 90-second readiness scorecard for VA + NC agents (12 questions across lead generation / listing presentation / niche calendar / operator inputs, returns a 0-100 readiness score plus top-three-gaps, captures email) turns the warm-but-not-yet-buyer agent audience into a tracked, tagged subscriber list.

Cost of Waiting

~$3-7K/mo in nurture-driven cohort and class-line fills not happening - ~$30-80K/yr compounding for life of the list you do not have. Every IG reel is a free public service until the scorecard ships. After it ships, every reel is a tracked lead-gen unit.

6 months from now -~$15-40K never captured plus the email list is 6 months smaller than it should be when the next cohort opens.

Fix - "Agent Leadership Readiness Score" scorecard on ScoreApp or Typeform. 3K-7K incremental Year-1 subscribers from the agent slice alone. $30-80K Year-1 lift from list-driven cohort fills.

Compound Pickup
04

Productise The Existing Online + In-Person Classes Into A Public Catalogue

The "online classes + in-person classes" line is already on the Facebook services tab. What is missing is the public catalogue. There is no published date list, no published price list, no published seat cap, no Calendly or Eventbrite or in-house booking layer that lets a prospective student see the next session and book a seat directly. Right now the class line operates as inquiry-based outreach. This is the slowest lever in the package - it does not move Year-1 revenue the way the cohort or the two scorecards do. It is on the list because it compounds. The fix is a two-track Day-30 install: (a) a public catalogue page with set dates, set prices, set seat caps, and a booking layer, and (b) tasting-portion class topics (listing-presentation walkthrough, MRP-niche calendar walkthrough, contract-negotiation walkthrough, social-media-for-realtors walkthrough) that compound the cohort funnel from the bottom up.

Cost of Waiting

~$400-1.6K/mo in single-session class bookings not captured - the $5-20K/yr compound-classes line stays untracked. Every Facebook services-tab visit, every IG bio click, every brokerage intake referral keeps the class line reading as inquiry-only rather than as a published, bookable surface.

12 months from now -Another year of the existing class delivery operating as inquiry-based instead of a public, bookable, cohort-feeding catalogue.

Fix - Classes productisation catalogue. Single-session formats at $97-$297 each. $5-20K Year-1 floor. Compounds as the cohort feeds the class catalogue with recurring single-session formats.
