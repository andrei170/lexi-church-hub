Copy Strategy - Lexi Church

Built: 2026-05-16 Frame: Voice, brand pillars, headline bank, and the framing AG1 will write to across the cohort landing, the two scorecards, the email sequences, and the hub deliverables.

Voice DNA

Lexi's public voice across Instagram, Facebook page tagline, LinkedIn headline, the WCR profile bio, and the FLOH Realty Group team-site framing:

  • "Real Take." The Facebook page tagline ("A Real Take on Real Estate") is the load-bearing brand line. The register is direct, declarative, candid - she tells you what is actually happening in the market, not what the trade press headlines say. Copy mirrors that register.
  • "Your Fav Realtor" warmth, layered on top of the operator register. The IG bio softens the directness with the friendliness that converts. Copy carries the warmth but never sacrifices the precision underneath.
  • Operator-grounded, not influencer-flat. Lexi is a working broker with $28M+ in stated career sales, a Local President role at Creed Realty, a First VP role at WCR, and a team she owns. She is monetising her own track record, not a brand-only persona. Copy treats her as the operator of the offer, not a mascot for it.
  • Leadership-tone, not coaching-circuit hype. The credential stack (Local President, First VP, founder + Team Owner, dual-state, MRP) is the brand's centre of gravity. Copy treats those credentials as observable fact, never as compliment, never as resume-flex.
  • Specialty-aware. Military families, first-time buyers, sellers from "cozy starter homes to high-end properties" - that range is Lexi's specialty span. Copy keeps the specialty span explicit on the consumer-facing surfaces and tightens it to the agent-leadership lane on the cohort-facing surfaces.

Brand pillars (what the copy stands on)

  1. Eight years on the lane, full-time, dual-state. The career arc is the cohort credibility. Copy cites the year-count and the dual-state license as observable fact.
  2. The credential stack speaks for itself. Local President at Creed Realty + First Vice President of Women's Council of REALTORS + founder + Team Owner of FLOH Realty Group + Military Relocation Professional + Associate Broker. Five credentials, one operator. Copy lists them clean, no flourish.
  3. "A Real Take on Real Estate." The Facebook page tagline is the brand line that travels across every surface. The cohort is the install version of that "Real Take" applied to agent career-building, not market-commentary.
  4. The 757 metro + the NC border-county lane. Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Suffolk, Chesapeake, Hampton, Newport News, plus the dual-state NC border counties. Copy keeps the geography explicit because the metro itself is the operating reality.
  5. The military-relocation specialty. MRP-credentialed in one of the largest military metros in the country. Copy treats this with respect and operational precision - it is a niche, not a hook.

What the cohort offer is, in one paragraph

A two-track cohort taught by Lexi Church for two parallel audiences inside the VA + NC real estate agent population. Track A is the licensed agent in years 1 to 3: lead-generation systems for the 757, listing-presentation playbook, contract-negotiation discipline, the military-relocation lane specifically (MRP-credentialed lane, base-cycle calendar awareness, VA-loan transaction muscle), social-media content cadence in the "Real Take" register, the first WCR or local-board leadership ladder to get on. 8 weeks. Track B is the mid-tenure agent in years 4 to 8: solo-to-team transition mechanics, brokerage-relationship architecture, recruiting newer agents under the team umbrella, the WCR leadership ladder as a brand-amplification asset, the brand-architecture work that turns a creator-account IG presence into a sustained referral engine. 8 weeks. Both tracks include weekly live calls with Lexi, weekly office hours, a private cohort room, and a final-week 1:1 review. Quarterly cadence. Caps at 20 (Track A) or 15 (Track B). The cohort is general real estate agent education and leadership coaching. Not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice. Results vary by market, capital, and operator execution.

Body voice rules

  • Open every section with a specific credential, a specific year-count, or a specific geography. "Local President at Creed Realty, First VP of WCR" reads stronger than "leader in the industry."
  • Cite the credentials by exact name. WCR. MRP. Associate Broker. Creed Realty Local President. FLOH Realty Group founder + Team Owner. The credential names are the brand asset.
  • Use "lane," "playbook," "system," "cadence," "track record," "operator," "council," "team," "brokerage," "listing presentation," "contract negotiation," "lead generation" generously - they are the cohort's vocabulary and the audience knows them.
  • Avoid "real estate millionaire," "passive income from real estate," "flip your way rich," "no-money-down strategies," "guaranteed transactions," "automatic referrals" - guru-circuit cliches that read instantly fake against Lexi's voice.
  • Avoid "transform your life," "unlock your potential," "level up your business" - banned across every AG1 copy surface.
  • Allow "build," "grow," "stack," "compound," "earn," "lead," "represent," "close" - the audience knows these terms and they signal precision.

Voice rules - DO NOT USE

This list ships only inside this strategy doc. It does not appear in research, vsl, email-sequence, or any client-facing surface.

  • "Game-changer" (banned everywhere by the copy-rules)
  • "Revolutionary," "unprecedented," "next level"
  • "Transform your life," "unlock your potential" (banned)
  • "In today's fast-paced world," "navigate the complexities," "delve into" (banned)
  • "Paradigm shift," "synergy" (banned)
  • "Passive income from real estate," "real estate millionaire mindset," "deals on autopilot," "set-and-forget income"
  • "The brokerages do not want you to know," "the secret most agents miss," "what they will not teach you in pre-licensing"
  • "Guaranteed X transactions," "guaranteed Y commission," "Z% close-rate guaranteed" - regulated-niche violations
  • "Lambo," "private jet," "luxury lifestyle" - aesthetic that does not match Lexi's voice
  • Specific transaction promises, specific commission predictions, specific market predictions for the participant
  • Em dashes - the entire package uses spaced hyphens or sentence rebuilds
  • "Took a fresh pass at the funnel" - banned phrase, use "studied your funnel this morning" or "made you a new plan since" instead

Outcomes language

Outcome-language is anchored to specific deliverables, not vague promises:

  • "By week 4 of Track A, you have a written lead-generation system mapped to the 757, a listing-presentation playbook drafted, and a first MRP-niche calendar laid out against the base-cycle calendar for VA + NC military installations."
  • "By week 8 of Track A, you have a written 12-month execution plan reviewed by Lexi on the 1:1 call, a closed listing-presentation rep cycle, and a council-leadership ladder mapped against the WCR + local-board calendar."
  • "By week 4 of Track B, you have a written team-build plan, a brokerage-relationship architecture mapped, and a written recruiting plan for the next 6-12 months."
  • "By week 8 of Track B, you have a written 12-month team-build execution plan reviewed by Lexi, a brand-architecture document mapping IG + Facebook + LinkedIn into a referral-engine system, and a written WCR leadership ladder pitch ready to deploy."

Promise-language to avoid:

  • "You will close X transactions in your first 90 days." (Promise, regulated-niche violation.)
  • "Earn $Y in commissions by graduation." (Income-claim, regulated-niche risk, false certainty.)
  • "Guaranteed team-build in 8 weeks." (False certainty.)

Comparison framing (against alternative offers)

The cohort competes with:

  • Tom Ferry coaching tiers ($5K-$30K range, national, group + 1:1 coaching). The 757 Agent Cohort beats Tom Ferry on geography-specificity (the 757 metro lane is taught by an operator who is in it, not from a national-coach perspective), on team-ownership specificity (Lexi has built FLOH, the Tom Ferry team-build coaching is more general), and on price-per-cohort-cycle.
  • Buffini & Company coaching ($4K-$12K range, national, referral-driven script-based coaching). The 757 Agent Cohort beats Buffini on contemporary content-cadence + social-media-system specificity, and on the dual-state + MRP-niche specificity.
  • Workman Success Systems coaching ($5K-$20K range, national, team-building focus). The 757 Agent Cohort beats Workman on the cohort dynamics (peer cohort of VA + NC agents working the same metro), and on the WCR leadership ladder integration.
  • Krista Mashore agent training tiers ($3K-$10K range, national, marketing-and-content focus). The 757 Agent Cohort beats Mashore on the council-leadership ladder integration, on the in-house team-ownership track, and on the MRP-niche specificity.
  • The existing online + in-person classes line on the Facebook services tab. The cohort sits above the classes in the ladder. The classes are single-session tasting-portions. The cohort is the 8-week structured curriculum with live access to Lexi each week.
  • Brokerage in-house training (Creed Realty's existing offerings). The cohort is brokerage-agnostic and complements rather than competes with brokerage-internal training. It is the leadership ladder, not the licence-and-onboarding ladder.
