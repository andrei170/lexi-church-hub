Positioning Statement

Lexi Church is Hampton Roads' military-family REALTOR. Associate Broker. MRP-certified. Dual-licensed VA + NC. Team founder. She operates in the metro that sits on the world's largest naval base. Eight years full-time. $28M+ closed. 21K IG. She is not another agent - she is the REALTOR who built a team in the market where the Navy never stops moving families.

The Bombshell

"You are the only Hampton Roads REALTOR with 21,000 followers AND a Military Relocation Professional certification AND a dual-state license covering both sides of the PCS corridor. The credential is real. The audience is real. The recurring pipeline of 5,000 military moves every PCS season is sitting completely ungated."

Primary Wedge

The Military Funnel Gap. The MRP designation without a productized funnel is a credential on a wall. John King at 757king.com built a dedicated PCS page. Jennifer Dawn built one. They are capturing every AI referral and every Google search from families PCSing to Norfolk, Virginia Beach, and the Hampton Roads corridor. Lexi has the IG audience they do not have. She is missing the infrastructure they built.

Voice Register

  • Warm but authoritative - Hampton Roads register, not stiff corporate
  • Accessible and real - "Your Fav Realtor" energy
  • Military-aware - uses PCS, BAH, VA loan as natural vocabulary, not jargon
  • Proud of the team she built - FLOH Realty Group is an identity, not a footnote
  • Mother of three - grounds her in the same life stage as many military spouse buyers

Key Proof Points (load-bearing, use verbatim)

  • 8 years full-time
  • $28M+ in sales
  • Military Relocation Professional (MRP) - NAR-certified
  • Dual-licensed Virginia + North Carolina
  • Associate Broker (above standard agent license)
  • Team Owner - FLOH Realty Group (For The Love Of Homes)
  • 21K Instagram followers
  • Women's Council of REALTORS Local President + National First Vice President

Tone Examples

DO: "When PCS orders hit, you need a REALTOR who already knows the base, the neighborhoods, and how VA loans work in this market." DO NOT: "Revolutionary AI-powered real estate experience."

DO: "Lexi built FLOH Realty Group so military families always have a team that shows up - on their timeline, not the market's." DO NOT: "Game-changing team of dedicated professionals."
