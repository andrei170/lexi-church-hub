Hey [First Name],

When a military family is coming to Hampton Roads, the first question is almost always: which side of the water?

Here is how I break it down:

If you're at NAS Oceana / Dam Neck: Virginia Beach proper. Ocean access. Commute is straightforward. Schools in the Kempsville and Princess Anne corridors are strong.

If you're at Naval Station Norfolk: Norfolk has pockets (Ghent, Larchmont) that are beautiful but pricier. Chesapeake right over the bridge gives you more house for your BAH with a short drive.

If you're at Joint Base Langley-Eustis / Fort Eustis: Hampton and Newport News. Completely different market from the Virginia Beach side - less saturated, more inventory, BAH stretches further.

If you're dual-station or PCSing through NC: My NC license covers you. Camp Lejeune area, Fort Liberty - I can work the full corridor.

Suffolk is the sleeper. If commute flexibility exists, the value per square foot in Suffolk is the best in the metro.
