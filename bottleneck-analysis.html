﻿# Bottleneck Analysis - Lexi Church / FLOH Realty Group Date: 2026-05-17

Bottleneck 1 - The Military Funnel Is Unplugged

The gap: Lexi holds the MRP (Military Relocation Professional) designation, operates in a metro with 100,000+ active-duty personnel across 19 installations, and serves the market next to the world's largest naval base. Yet there is no dedicated military landing page, no lead magnet, and no email nurture sequence for PCS-bound families.

What it costs: ~5,000 military members transfer in/out of Hampton Roads annually. PCS season (Feb-Aug) produces a recurring, predictable wave of motivated buyers. Every family who Googles "REALTOR Virginia Beach military PCS" instead of finding Lexi finds John King or Jennifer Dawn.

The fix: Build one page. Create one lead magnet (Hampton Roads PCS Move Guide PDF). Put it behind an email capture. Run IG content pointing to it. The credential is already there - it just needs a funnel attached.

Impact: HIGH - This is the primary revenue-unlocking fix.

Bottleneck 2 - AEO Score 0/5

The gap: AI-sourced referrals (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) are replacing the first page of search for real estate queries. For every query Lexi should appear in - "best military relocation REALTOR Virginia Beach," "Hampton Roads luxury real estate," "PCS REALTOR Hampton Roads," "FLOH Realty Group real estate" - she is not cited.

Competitors who are cited: John King (757king.com), Jennifer Dawn Real Estate, McKone Group, Broadsight Realty, TREG - all have basic MRP-framed web pages that AI engines extract.

What it costs: Every month of zero AEO presence is lost referral traffic from the highest-intent buyers in the market (military families with firm orders = non-negotiable timeline = motivated buyers).

The fix: One well-structured page with schema markup, FAQ schema, and military-specific copy earns citations within 30-60 days. Lexi's MRP credential + dual-state license is citation-worthy content - it just needs to be on a crawlable page.

Impact: HIGH - Compounds over time. First-mover advantage in the military-niche AEO lane is still available.

Bottleneck 3 - SSL/Site Technical Issues

The gap: flohrealtygroup.com has a TLS certificate alt-name error on the www subdomain. Any warm prospect who Googles FLOH Realty Group after receiving an IG DM or seeing a post encounters a browser security warning. lexichurch.com returned ECONNREFUSED during site crawl - either JS-gated, Cloudflare-protected, or intermittently down.

What it costs: A REALTOR whose brand page throws a security warning is not signing luxury buyers. Trust is the product in real estate. A broken SSL on the team brand site is a conversion killer.

The fix: Fix the SSL cert on flohrealtygroup.com (15-minute server-side update). Confirm lexichurch.com loads cleanly for mobile users arriving from IG bio link.

Impact: MEDIUM - Blocking issue for warm traffic. Low effort to fix.

Bottleneck 4 - No Team-Recruitment Funnel

The gap: Lexi is an Associate Broker and Team Owner of FLOH Realty Group. This is a rare credential - she can legally recruit and supervise agents, operate a team brand, and build a brokerage-within-a-brokerage. Zero indexed content targets agent recruitment. No "Join FLOH" page. No "Why I started this team" content. No agent testimonials.

What it costs: Every licensed agent in Hampton Roads who wants a team environment is a potential FLOH recruit. Team growth means revenue share, increased production volume, and brand scale without Lexi being on every single transaction.

The fix: One "Join FLOH Realty Group" page with Lexi's story, her coaching philosophy, her volume track record, and a simple application form. Feed it with IG content from the FLOH/team-building pillar.

Impact: MEDIUM - Not the first priority, but the longest-duration ROI lever in the package.

Bottleneck 5 - No Email List

The gap: 21,000 IG followers. Zero owned email list (based on no evidence of lead capture on either site). All audience depends on Instagram's algorithm, which changes, restricts organic reach, and can shadowban without warning.

What it costs: A single algorithm update or account flag removes 21,000 touchpoints overnight. There is no fallback.

The fix: The PCS Move Guide lead magnet (Bottleneck 1) doubles as the email list seed. One lead magnet, two problems solved simultaneously. An email list of 500 warm, PCS-motivated subscribers is worth more in transaction pipeline than 21,000 passive followers.

Impact: HIGH (solved concurrently with Bottleneck 1)

Priority Order

  1. Build the military-niche landing page + PCS Move Guide lead magnet + email capture (solves B1 and B5 simultaneously)
  2. Add FAQ schema and MRP-framed copy to the new page (solves B2 - AEO citations)
  3. Fix SSL on flohrealtygroup.com (solves B3 - low effort, high trust impact)
  4. Build "Join FLOH" team recruitment page (solves B4 - longer-horizon play)
