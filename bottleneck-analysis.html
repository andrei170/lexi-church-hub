Bottleneck Analysis - Lexi Church

Built: 2026-05-16 Frame: Cold first-touch. No prior outreach. This is the structural map - the four things I would build first if I were stepping into the Lexi Church funnel today. Scope: The gap between a stacked-leadership-credential agent (Local President at Creed Realty, First Vice President of Women's Council of REALTORS, founder + Team Owner of FLOH Realty Group, 8 years full-time, dual-licensed VA + NC, MRP) with a 21K-follower Instagram audience and the productised agent-leadership cohort the credential stack is implicitly already advertising but is not publicly bookable.

The one-sentence diagnosis

The Lexi Church ladder is essentially one step deep on the consumer side (FLOH Realty Group brokerage transactions) and one step deep on the agent side (informally-delivered "online classes + in-person classes" listed on the Facebook services tab with no productised cadence, no public pricing, no application gate), with the productised agent-leadership cohort the credential stack is implicitly pre-selling sitting empty. The cohort is the wedge.

The four things I would build first

One. The 757 Agent Cohort - the productised agent-leadership lane behind the credential stack.

The current funnel offers two endpoints. Free Instagram + Facebook + LinkedIn content for the audience following Lexi for "Real Take" market commentary and leadership content at the top. The FLOH Realty Group brokerage intake on the consumer side (buy-side and list-side transactions across the 757 metro plus the NC border counties). The "online classes + in-person classes" line is listed on the Facebook services tab but no publicly-bookable productised surface for it exists. Cohort-shaped agent education with weekly live calls + office hours + an application gate is not visible publicly.

Everyone in the 21K-follower audience who has watched the leadership content, the Creed Realty Local President framing, the WCR First VP credential, and the FLOH Realty Group team-build content - and wants the playbook applied to their own first-three-years or first-team-build stage - has nowhere productised to land. The contact form is the consumer brokerage form, not an agent-leadership application.

A productised mid-tier closes the gap. Two parallel cohorts, both taught live by Lexi:

  • The 757 Agent Cohort - Track A (First Three Years In The Lane). 8 weeks. For licensed agents in VA or NC in years 1 to 3. Modules: lead-generation systems for the 757 metro (and the NC border counties), listing-presentation playbook, contract-negotiation discipline, the military-relocation lane specifically (MRP-credentialed lane, base-cycle calendar awareness, VA-loan transaction muscle), social-media content cadence in the "Real Take" register, the first WCR or local-board leadership ladder to get on. Caps at 20. Quarterly cadence. Mid-four-figure range.

  • The 757 Agent Cohort - Track B (From Solo Producer To Team Owner). 8 weeks. For mid-tenure agents in years 4 to 8 who are producing but plateauing. Modules: solo-to-team transition mechanics (what FLOH Realty Group actually looks like operationally), brokerage-relationship architecture (Creed Realty as a reference template, not a recruiting ask), recruiting newer agents under the team umbrella, the WCR leadership ladder as a brand-amplification asset, brand-architecture work that turns a creator-account IG presence into a sustained referral engine. Caps at 15. Quarterly cadence. Low-five-figure range.

Year-1 floor (cohort revenue only):

  • Track A: 4 cycles x 20 seats x $3,000 = $240,000
  • Track B: 4 cycles x 15 seats x $7,000 = $420,000
  • Combined direct: $660,000

That is on top of existing FLOH Realty Group brokerage commission revenue and the existing informal "online classes + in-person classes" line. The conservatism is deliberate: prices are floor numbers, the fill rate assumed is 75 to 80% of cap, and the comparable agent-coaching programs (Tom Ferry coaching tiers $5K-$30K, Buffini & Company coaching $4K-$12K, Workman Success Systems coaching $5K-$20K, Krista Mashore agent training tiers $3K-$10K) price the operator-track equivalent meaningfully higher than the cohort floor used here. The floor reads understated on purpose for the first year while the cohort builds testimonial volume. The cohort is general real estate agent education and leadership coaching. Not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice. Results vary by market, capital, and operator execution.

Two. The Agent Leadership Readiness Score - the agent-side scorecard that turns the IG + LinkedIn audience into a tracked email list.

There is no visible Lexi-owned email list. The bio link on Instagram, the contact line on Facebook, the LinkedIn headline - none of them capture an email into a tracked, segmented list owned by Lexi. There is no quiz, no scorecard, no PDF download, no calculator on any public surface for the agent-side audience.

Every IG follower who watches a leadership reel, gets pulled in by the Local President + First VP + founder framing, and lands on the bio link exits the funnel without entering a list that can be nurtured back into the cohort.

The fix is a single converting asset that does double duty:

"Agent Leadership Readiness Score" - a 90-second readiness scorecard for VA + NC agents. 12 questions across four dimensions: lead-generation cadence (weekly hours on outreach, pipeline density, source mix), listing-presentation muscle (presentations per month, conversion rate, contract-to-close rate), niche calendar awareness (MRP / first-time-buyer / luxury / move-up / military-base-cycle awareness, council-leadership ladder position), operator inputs (years licensed, team membership, hours per week available, capital available for systems). Returns a 0-100 readiness score, top-three-gaps email follow-up. Captures email, segments by score band and by years-licensed band.

Conservative IG-and-LinkedIn-to-email conversion on a 21K-follower IG audience plus the LinkedIn leadership thread plus the FB cross-post engine is 4-7% inside a 90-day window. The agent slice of that audience is roughly 30 to 50% of the total follower base (the rest being consumer + WCR peer follows), so the realistic Year-1 capture range is 3K-7K incremental email subscribers from the agent slice alone - segmented and tagged at the moment of capture.

That list is the asset that turns every future cohort cycle from "depends on IG algorithm" to "open the list, nurture, fill." The cohort is general real estate agent education and leadership coaching. Not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice. Results vary by market, capital, and operator execution.

Year-1 floor on the agent scorecard:

  • Cohort fills sourced from the list (compounds each cycle): $20K-$60K direct lift over the year
  • The existing class-line bookings from list-nurture sequences: $10K-$20K direct lift
  • Combined incremental agent-scorecard floor: $30K-$80K Year-1

Email economics are the part that compounds. Every IG reel that ships into a warm list converts at a multiple of cold. This is not a one-time lift.

Three. The 757 Home Sale Readiness Score - the consumer-side scorecard that routes the home-seller + home-buyer slice into FLOH Realty Group brokerage intake.

Separate from the agent-side scorecard. The consumer slice of the 21K-follower IG audience is the 757 + NC border-county home seller + home buyer + military-relocation-family who follows Lexi for the "Real Take" market commentary. That slice is currently converting into FLOH Realty Group transactions only through the brokerage intake form on the team site - no warm-audience scorecard, no list capture, no segmented nurture into the brokerage funnel.

The fix is a parallel scorecard sized to the consumer side:

"757 Home Sale Readiness Score" - a 90-second readiness scorecard for VA + NC home sellers + buyers. 10 questions across three dimensions: home equity position + selling timeline (current value estimate, mortgage payoff position, target close window), motivation + financing (move-up / downsize / relocation / military-orders trigger, financing position, preferred close timeline), micro-market clarity (which 757 sub-market the home sits in, comparable-sale awareness, broker preference signals). Returns a 0-100 readiness score, top-three-gaps email follow-up. Captures email + routes ready-to-transact respondents to the FLOH Realty Group intake form.

Conservative IG-and-Facebook-to-email conversion on the consumer slice of the audience is 4-6%, returning 1K-3K incremental Year-1 email subscribers. The slice that converts to a brokerage transaction inside the 12-to-18-month attention window depends on average sale price and FLOH commission structure (both private to Lexi), but the routing math is straightforward.

Year-1 floor on the consumer scorecard:

  • Incremental brokerage transactions sourced from the scorecard email list (compounds across calendar year): $50K-$150K incremental commission revenue Year-1
  • Floor anchored to the $28M+ stated career-sales-volume signal and conservative attribution of incremental transactions to the new capture surface
  • Combined incremental consumer-scorecard floor: $50K-$150K Year-1

This is the lever that directly increases FLOH Realty Group brokerage revenue without changing the brokerage operations - it routes the warm-but-not-tracked consumer slice into the existing intake.

Four. Productise the existing online + in-person classes into a public catalogue with set dates, set prices, set seat caps, and a recurring booking surface.

The "online classes + in-person classes" line is already on the Facebook services tab. What is missing is the public catalogue. There is no published date list, no published price list, no published seat cap, no Calendly or Eventbrite or in-house booking layer that lets a prospective student see the next session and book a seat directly.

Right now the class line operates as inquiry-based outreach. The fix is to convert it into a public-pricing, set-cadence catalogue that feeds the cohort funnel from the bottom up.

The mechanic:

  • Class catalogue page sits on lexichurch.com or flohrealtygroup.com with a published list of the next 90 days of sessions, each with title + date + format (online or in-person) + price + seat cap + booking button
  • Booking layer (Calendly + Stripe or Eventbrite) handles the bookings end-to-end with no email-back-and-forth
  • Class topics that compound into the cohort - "Listing Presentation Live Walkthrough" (single 2-hour session), "Contract Negotiation Live Walkthrough" (single 2-hour session), "MRP Niche Calendar Walkthrough" (single 2-hour session), "Social Media For Realtors Live Walkthrough" (single 2-hour session). Each class is a tasting-portion of the cohort curriculum, priced at $97-$297, with the cohort upgrade-CTA on every confirmation page

This is the slowest lever in the package - it does not move Year-1 revenue the way the cohort, the agent scorecard, or the consumer scorecard do. It is on the list because:

  • The class line is already a stated service offering on the Facebook page (it just is not productised)
  • Single-session class economics compound the cohort funnel (every student who books a class becomes a warm cohort applicant)
  • The class catalogue page is a load-bearing SEO asset that captures "real estate agent training Virginia Beach" and "MRP training VA" search queries that currently route to nothing Lexi owns

Year-1 floor on the classes productisation:

  • Single-session class bookings at $97-$297 each, conservative monthly cadence of 2-4 sessions, conservative fill rate of 6-12 students per session: $5K-$20K direct revenue
  • Cohort applicants sourced from class confirmation pages (incremental, beyond the scorecard routing): captured in the cohort Year-1 floor
  • Combined incremental classes-productisation floor: $5K-$20K Year-1

This is the lever that does not move quickly but cleans up the bottom of the funnel and turns the existing informal class delivery into a public, bookable, recurring revenue line that feeds the cohort.
