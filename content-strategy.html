Phase 3

Content Strategy - Lexi Church

Built: 2026-05-16 Frame: Content lanes that feed The 757 Agent Cohort (Track A + Track B), the two scorecard-driven email assets (Agent Leadership Readiness Score + 757 Home Sale Readiness Score), and the existing FLOH Realty Group brokerage intake. The goal is to convert the 21K Instagram + Facebook + LinkedIn audience into segmented email subscribers without slowing the existing IG cadence or diluting the "A Real Take on Real Estate" brand pillar.

Voice anchors

  • "Real Take," not market-puff. The Facebook tagline is the load-bearing brand line. The register is direct, candid, declarative - the kind of read most agents will not publish because it is too specific. Specifics earn trust in a niche oversaturated with happy-talk.
  • "Your Fav Realtor" warmth, layered on top. The IG bio softens the directness with the friendliness that converts. Copy carries the warmth but never sacrifices the precision underneath.
  • Operator-grounded, leadership-tone. The credential stack (Local President at Creed Realty, First VP at WCR, founder + Team Owner of FLOH, dual-state, MRP, 8 years full-time, $28M+ stated career sales) is the brand's centre of gravity. Reference the credentials as observable fact, never as resume-flex.
  • Specialty-aware. Military families, first-time buyers, sellers from "cozy starter homes to high-end properties." Range is preserved on consumer surfaces, tightened to the agent-leadership lane on cohort-facing surfaces.
  • Regulated-niche guard rails. Every content unit that touches market predictions, commission outcomes, transaction guarantees, or transaction-readiness includes the implicit "this is general real estate guidance / general agent education, not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice" framing. The explicit footer ships on every owned long-form caption.

Content pillars (four)

Pillar one - the 757 market read ("Real Take")

Topic territory: candid micro-market commentary on the 757 metro (Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Suffolk, Chesapeake, Hampton, Newport News) plus the NC border counties. What is actually moving. What is not. What the comparable-sales mix is saying that the headline numbers are not. Mortgage-rate impact on the current absorption rate. Inventory trends. The specific sub-markets that are tightening or loosening.

Why this pillar matters: this is the consumer-side top-of-funnel for the FLOH Realty Group brokerage engine. Every clip here is a routing asset for the 757 Home Sale Readiness Score. Direct top-of-funnel for FLOH brokerage transactions.

Sample hook formats:

  • "Real take on the Virginia Beach market this week. The number the headlines are not citing."
  • "Which 757 sub-market just shifted. Three comps from the last 30 days."
  • "Mortgage-rate impact on absorption in Norfolk this quarter. Real numbers, real context."
  • "What the listing-cancellation rate is telling us about seller-side motivation right now."

Pillar two - the military-relocation lane (MRP-niche)

Topic territory: the operational reality of buying and selling around the 757 military base cycle. Naval Station Norfolk + Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek + Naval Air Station Oceana + the Marine Corps installations. PCS season timing. VA-loan transaction muscle. The specific differences between civilian-buy and military-relocation-buy transactions. The agent-side play (MRP credential, base-cycle calendar awareness) and the consumer-side play (working with an MRP-credentialed agent who knows the calendar).

Why this pillar matters: this is the cross-over pillar - it serves both the consumer slice (military families relocating into the 757) and the agent slice (newer VA + NC agents who want to learn the military-relocation lane). Routes warm traffic to both scorecards.

Sample hook formats:

  • "PCS season is opening. Three things the seller-side needs to know about military-buyer timelines."
  • "VA-loan transaction muscle. The specific approvals that move faster, the specific ones that do not."
  • "MRP-credentialed agent vs general agent. The actual operational difference inside a military-relocation transaction."
  • "Base-cycle calendar for the 757 - 757 military installations and the months that drive the relocation flow."

Pillar three - the agent-leadership lane (cohort top-of-funnel)

Topic territory: the work of building a real estate career from licence through first-team-owner. Lead-generation systems. Listing-presentation muscle. Contract-negotiation discipline. The WCR + local-board leadership ladder. The decision points that take an agent from year 1 to year 3 to year 8. What the credential stack actually does for the brand. The story of FLOH Realty Group as a reference template.

Why this pillar matters: this is the cohort-pitch lane. Every clip in this pillar is teaching the agent-slice of the audience "you can do this" via case-study evidence. The cohort productises pillar three into curriculum.

Sample hook formats:

  • "First three years in real estate. The decision that moved my pipeline the hardest."
  • "Local President at a brokerage and First VP at the Council. The credential ladder, in order."
  • "What the WCR leadership ladder actually does for a producer-agent's brand."
  • "Solo agent to team owner. The three operational shifts most agents underestimate."

Pillar four - the FLOH Realty Group team-build lane (Track B top-of-funnel)

Topic territory: the operational reality of running a team brokerage in the 757. Hiring the first agent. Splits-and-structure decisions. The CRM + listing-pipeline tech stack. Recruiting newer agents under the team umbrella. The brand-architecture work that turns a creator-account IG presence into a sustained referral engine. The Creed Realty parent-brokerage relationship as a reference template.

Why this pillar matters: this is the Track B top-of-funnel - mid-tenure agents at the plateau watching to figure out what team-ownership actually looks like. Routes warm traffic to the Agent Leadership Readiness Score with Track B framing.

Sample hook formats:

  • "First agent I hired into FLOH. The interview question that mattered most."
  • "Splits-and-structure decisions for the first three agents on a team. What I would and would not do again."
  • "Recruiting newer agents into a team umbrella. The signals I read in the first 30 minutes."
  • "Brand-architecture for a small team. Three lanes that compound the referral engine."

Atomisation playbook

Every long-form piece (one LinkedIn long-form, one IG carousel, one team-site blog post) is repurposable into 6-12 distinct content units. Default atomisation per long-form unit:

  • 3-5 IG reel clips (45-90 sec each), captioned, branded
  • 2-3 short-form Facebook posts pulling specific examples
  • 1-2 LinkedIn carousels pulling the strongest framework
  • 1 newsletter recap (post-install) pointing to the full piece
  • 1 team-site blog post anchoring the long-form source

This turns one piece of long-form into 7-10 days of social content across four surfaces without filming additional units.

Scorecard quiz - distribution plan

Once the two scorecards ship (Agent Leadership Readiness Score + 757 Home Sale Readiness Score), every piece of content gets a single CTA routed by audience slice:

  • Agent-side content (pillars three + four + the agent-leaning slice of pillar two) routes to the Agent Leadership Readiness Score
  • Consumer-side content (pillar one + the consumer-leaning slice of pillar two) routes to the 757 Home Sale Readiness Score
  • Cross-over content (military-relocation lane in pillar two) gets routed by audience-slice framing in the caption

That single funnel decision - scorecard first, cohort second (agent side) or scorecard first, brokerage intake second (consumer side) - is the structural unlock. Until it is built, every IG reel is a free public service. After it ships, every IG reel is a tracked lead-gen unit. The cohort is general real estate agent education and leadership coaching. Not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice. Results vary by market, capital, and operator execution.

What to stop posting

  • Generic "be your fav realtor" content without a "Real Take" specific
  • Market-puff that does not cite specific comps or specific sub-market shifts
  • "Become a real estate millionaire" framings (banned)
  • Specific predictions about home prices, mortgage rates, or market direction (regulated-niche risk)
  • Content that hides the operational mechanics behind aesthetic
  • Anything that frames the audience as if they need to be sold (the cohort audience needs to be educated and qualified, not sold)

What to start posting

  • Behind-the-scenes of an actual listing presentation (anonymised, real numbers framed in ranges)
  • Behind-the-scenes of an MRP-credentialed military-relocation transaction (anonymised, with operational detail)
  • Build-in-public of the cohort during the first 1-2 cycles
  • Cohort-grad case studies once the first 1-2 cycles have completed (anonymised initially, named with permission later)
  • Real-take micro-market reads with three specific comps each
  • WCR + Creed Realty leadership-room takeaways (anonymised, with the takeaway-substance preserved)

Measurement

Track four metrics weekly: (1) Scorecard A starts (agent side), (2) Scorecard A-to-email completion rate, (3) Scorecard B starts (consumer side), (4) Scorecard B-to-email completion rate plus brokerage intake routing rate. Everything else is secondary. The funnel either converts or does not at these four gates.

Compliance posture

Where the content touches market predictions, transaction outcomes, commission outcomes, or specific home-buying / home-selling decisions, the format is "real number, real context, individual case." No generalised "you can make $X in commissions" framing. The implicit message is "this is what worked for me / for this cohort grad / for this transaction" - never an extrapolated guarantee for the viewer. Every long-form caption and every cohort-related short-form caption carries the line: "Not personalised real-estate, legal, or financial advice. Results vary by market, capital, and operator execution." This is non-negotiable. The cohort is general real estate agent education and leadership coaching, not personalised real-estate, legal, tax, or financial advice. Specific home-buying, home-selling, financing, tax, and legal decisions should always be made in consultation with the appropriate licensed professional.
