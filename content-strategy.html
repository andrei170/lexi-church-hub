Content Pillars (IG Primary)

Pillar 1 - Military Family Education (40%)

The core authority pillar. Lexi owns the PCS-to-Hampton-Roads niche better than anyone in the market when she activates it.

  • "What BAH covers in Virginia Beach right now" (updated quarterly)
  • "The 5 neighborhoods closest to NAS Oceana for military families"
  • "VA loan myths that cost military buyers their dream home"
  • "PCS timeline: when to start talking to a REALTOR before your orders arrive"
  • "Norfolk Naval Station vs Langley AFB: which side of Hampton Roads is better for your family?"
  • "Renting vs buying on a PCS: the honest math for Hampton Roads 2026"

Pillar 2 - Hampton Roads Local Life (25%)

Builds the lifestyle authority that attracts non-military buyers and keeps general audience engaged.

  • Virginia Beach neighborhood spotlights (Chesapeake, Suffolk, Hampton, Newport News)
  • "What $400K buys you in Virginia Beach right now"
  • Restaurant/lifestyle content ("Your Fav Realtor's guide to the best spots near base")
  • School district breakdowns for military families with kids
  • Hampton Roads hidden gems for new-to-area families

Pillar 3 - Behind FLOH Realty Group (20%)

Team owner content. Positions Lexi as a leader, not just an agent. Attracts agent recruits and builds brand depth.

  • "Why I started FLOH Realty Group"
  • Agent day-in-the-life content
  • "What I look for when I bring someone onto the FLOH team"
  • Team milestone celebrations (closings, anniversaries, agent achievements)
  • Women's Council of REALTORS content (Local President - underused credibility)

Pillar 4 - Social Proof and Client Stories (15%)

Real client wins. Prioritize military family testimonials.

  • PCS success stories ("They got orders in April. They were in their new home by June.")
  • First-time buyer wins
  • Luxury/move-up seller stories
  • Before/after of FLOH listings
